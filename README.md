SGX STI 30 Daily Digest

Automated executive digest of company announcements from the 30 constituents of Singapore's Straits Times Index daily.

**→ Read the latest digest: https://jquarticai.github.io/sgx-sti30-digest/**

## What this is

Each day, a pipeline pulls company announcement filings from the [Singapore Exchange](https://www.sgx.com/securities/company-announcements), filters them down to the 30 STI constituents, classifies each one as high-priority or routine, and writes a roughly 100-word plain-English summary. The result is a single self-contained HTML page per day, plus an index of the archive.

The digest refreshes daily.

## Methodology

**Source.** All filings are pulled directly from the official SGX announcements API (`api.sgx.com/announcements`). Each summary card links back to the original filing on `links.sgx.com` — the original is always the authoritative version.

**Classification.**

- **High priority** — financial results, profit warnings, M&A activity, board or C-suite changes, special dividends materially above or below expectation, rights issues, regulatory actions, trading halts, defaults.
- **Routine** — daily share buy-back notices, AGM/EGM minutes, in-line dividend declarations, employee share scheme treasury-share use, director dealings, administrative replacements.

A filing tagged "routine" is routine in form, not necessarily unimportant. Use the summary to triage, then click through to read the actual filing.

## Disclaimer

These summaries are generated by an AI pipeline. They may contain errors, omit material context, or misclassify the priority of a filing. **They are not financial advice, investment recommendations, or a substitute for reading the original SGX filing.** Before acting on anything you read here, verify against the linked source document.

The summaries reflect a snapshot at the time of generation. Filings can be amended, withdrawn, or superseded — only the live SGX record is authoritative.
