General Announcement — Keppel Sakra Cogen Plant commences commercial operations
Keppel's 600MW Keppel Sakra Cogen (KSC) Plant, Singapore's first hydrogen-compatible combined-cycle gas turbine, began commercial operations today, expanding Keppel's power capacity by about 45% to roughly 1,900MW (combined with Merlimau Cogen), supplemented by up to 200MW of imported low-carbon power. The plant is fully contracted for 2026 and 2027, bolstering recurring income. KSC is 70% owned by Keppel Asia Infrastructure Fund and 30% by Keppel, reflecting Keppel's asset-light manager-operator model. It can co-fire up to 30% hydrogen with natural gas at the onset and could reduce up to 220,000 tonnes of CO2 annually versus Singapore's grid average.
